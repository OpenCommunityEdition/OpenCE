# Authors

## Art

- **The game's icon** (the Android app's launcher icon, `halo.exe`'s icon,
  the desktop windows' icon and the Mac app's icon, `port/mac/app/OpenCE.icns`,
  which is this picture in the icns format): by ORION (`port/assets/icon`).
- **The controller's A, B, X and Y buttons** (the menus' button icons and
  the HUD messages'): redraws by saulob (`port/assets/buttons`).
- **The high-res HUD** (its meters, icons, scopes and messages, and the
  weapons' reticles): redraws by MrBruh (`port/assets/hud`), based on
  reference photos by Aerocatia. The sniper rifle's zoom labels (2x, 10x)
  are set in Overpass (`port/assets/fonts`).
- **The menus' mouse pointer** (the arrowhead and chevrons the menus draw
  under the mouse): drawn in code by OpenCE contributors
  (`port/linux/game/menu_cursor.c`).
- **The settings pictures** (the nine redraws of the pictures beside the
  multiplayer type, gametype and profile screens' settings, which the menus
  draw when the player's own install has none): by OpenCE contributors
  (`port/assets/menus/svg/shell/main_menu`).
- **The Mac's reduced HUD** (the high-res HUD scaled down for the Mac's
  small video memory): reductions by OpenCE contributors of MrBruh's redraws
  (`port/assets/hud_mac`, made by `tools/mac_hud_reduce.py`).

## Third-party art

- **Voice chat's speaker icons** (who talks, and who is muted, in the lobby,
  on the scoreboard and in the game): `volume-2` and `volume-x` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, copyright (c) 2026 Lucide Icons and
  Contributors, under the ISC licence
  (`port/assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE`).
- **The Mods menu's warning icons** (a mod out of its requested order, and
  a mod that cannot be played): `triangle-alert` and `circle-x` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, under the same licence.
