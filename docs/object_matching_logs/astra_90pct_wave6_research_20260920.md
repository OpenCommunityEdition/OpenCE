## Why strict gains remain small

Earlier waves often improved evidence or source fidelity without making every instruction and relocation exact. High similarity is dominated by allocation, scheduling and ownership differences that a local correction does not necessarily resolve. Some apparent opportunities were already accepted; others relied on forbidden undefined behavior or metadata assumptions. ARGB demonstrates the remaining cost directly: a credible exact helper still cannot be credited when its real visibility changes an inherited caller. The supplied later builds expose useful operations and aggregate extents, but their differing bodies and missing January lexical/type records cannot establish a general compiler law.

Wave 6's vertex closure succeeds where those broader attempts did not because a specific missing API operation is independently supported, naturally specializes under the authentic Xbox SDK, and survives strict controls. It adds only its verified 354 meaningful bytes. These limits guide the next evidence search; they do not establish that the 90% objective is impossible.
