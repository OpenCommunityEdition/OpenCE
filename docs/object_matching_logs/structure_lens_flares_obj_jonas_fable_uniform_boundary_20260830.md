## Fail-closed rejection and restored boundary

The frozen baseline manifest is 485,380 bytes with SHA-256
`8a2388e63f898d762e39de269cb758f7bb8daf43502f5bd5aa029349a00847b9`.
The candidate check rejected the changed physical section/symbol ownership:
the three accepted sibling identities became `UNKNOWN`, the target-owned
`.rdata` identities and `.debug$F` changed, and the whole symbol set changed.
Direct function comparison is not authority to waive those ownership failures.

The source was restored exactly without testing a second production spelling.
After rebuilding the restored blob, the same manifest passes with no failures
or warnings and lists all three siblings in `still_exact`.

The remaining boundary is unchanged in kind. Seven January conversions are
inline expansions of the historical assembly `fast_ftol`; ordinary C under
VC7 13.00.9254 emits seven `_ftol2` calls under the proven uniform flags. The
new plane/loop topology is worth reconsidering only if a future candidate can
preserve the complete physical TU ownership manifest. Do not retry the Fable
casts, `/QIfist`, inline assembly, the same topology stack, or source-order
variants without new ownership evidence.
