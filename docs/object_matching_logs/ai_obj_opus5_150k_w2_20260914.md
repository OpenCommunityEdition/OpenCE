## Per-function outcomes

### `_ai_test_ballistic_line_of_fire`: not landed (x87 split-enregistration residual)

**What January does** (0x1e7-0x237, 0x2cc):

1. `fsubr [end]` computes `segment_time` inside `segment_start_time`'s x87 slot.
2. `fst [ebp+0x14]` gives it a memory home and keeps it on the stack.
3. The x and y rows use `fld [i|j]; fmul st(1)`.
4. The z statement is `fld [ebp+0x14]; fxch st(1); fmul [k]; fadd [p.z]; fld st(1); fmul st(2); fmul [gravity];
   fmul [0.5]; faddp; fstp [z]; fstp st(0)`. The held copy is consumed by the `k` term, and the reloaded copy feeds
   the square.
5. The velocity update after the collision calls reloads `[ebp+0x14]`. A scan found only one definition of that
   slot.

**What ours does:** `fld [end]; fsub st(1); fstp [ebp+0xc]; fstp st(0)`, then memory operands for every use. The
homes of `collision_flags` and `segment_time` are swapped.

**New lab measurements.** The micro TU in `scratch/workers/ai/lab/gen.py` models the loop with the ai.obj flags. It
reproduces the current candidate's codegen for this region instruction for instruction, and also wave D's
factored `bal1` hold.

- **L-A.** A named `real` local defined in a loop is held on the x87 stack only when every use is call-free. One use
  after a CALL makes it memory-only (fstp home, memory operands). None of these changed that:
  - 0 to 6 extra call-free uses;
  - 1 to 3 post-call uses;
  - function or loop scope;
  - `real const`;
  - a split z statement;
  - an inline parameter temporary (it is copy-propagated).
- **L-B.** Spelling the post-call use as the defining expression `(segment_end_time - segment_start_time)` lets VC7
  CSE it with the definition. This reproduces January's `fsubr; fst [home]; fmul st(1)` prefix. The z statement,
  however, then feeds the square from the held value (`fld [k]; fmul st(1) ... fld st(1); fmul st(2)`), not from
  January's `fld [home]; fxch st(1)`. The spelling is not natural source either, because it recomputes a named
  in-scope value.
- **L-C.** A 128-variant sweep crossed named/unnamed spellings of x, y, the z `k` term, both square factors and the
  velocity update with two term orders (`sweep1.txt`). A further 66 variants (`v12`-`v15`) tried operand order,
  constant order, placement and velocity-update targets. None reproduces January's z statement.
- **L-D.** `#pragma optimize` variants `p`, `a`, `w`, `y`, `t` and `g` reproduce nothing. `s` gives a different split
  (`fst; fstp st(1)`). These were diagnostics only; no pragma is proposed.
- **Donor scan** (`scratch/workers/ai/scan/fstscan2.py`, all January split objects). The "fst home + cross-statement
  st(n) hold + later reload" pattern occurs in 15 January functions:
  - **3 are strict-exact in our tree:** `_render_contrail` (`width`), antenna `code_00120c40` (`scale`) and
    `_build_distant_lights`. In each, the held copy only feeds the statements right after the definition. None
    has the in-place subtract plus post-call reload shape.
  - **The other 12 are not exact:** ballistic itself, 10 more with differing instruction streams, and
    `_structure_test_pill2d`, which is absent from our build. They are listed under "Research lead" below.

**Classification:** x87 split enregistration of `segment_time`, with no natural spelling found.

**Reopen criterion:** either of the following.
- A same-compiler exact donor in which a named real local is held on the x87 stack across two or more statements
  and reloaded after a CALL.
- A natural spelling that reproduces L-B's prefix and also January's `fld [home]; fxch st(1)` z statement.

### `_ai_test_line_of_fire`: skipped, exhausted (admissibility-blocked)

Wave D's `lof2` (bare `(pills[pill_index].width)` plus the corrected store order) is strict exact. The orchestrator
rejected it because no genuine macro spells the parentheses. No such macro was found:

- no project header, `cseries.h` or `real_math.h` has one;
- Stian, punpckhdq and HCEA have none (the HCEA `?:` form is inert).

Prototype-level explanations are refuted by the January bytes and relocations:

- The call site is cdecl: `add esp,0x10` and an undecorated symbol.
- The argument is a 4-byte `fstp`, so there is no K&R or varargs double promotion.
- `real const` and non-inlined `__inline` callee forms are inert.

**Reopen criterion (unchanged):** an attested or owner-approved parenthesising spelling for
`fast_vector_intersects_sphere` radius arguments. That spelling would also address players, collisions and bipeds.

### `_ai_test_line_of_sight`: skipped, exhausted (policy-blocked)

Re-verified from January's bytes:

- The clear path (0x10d) stores only the clear flag.
- The hit path copies `collision.t` into `[ebp-0xc]`.
- The fog > 0.8 path reaches the distance classifier regardless of the clear flag and reads `[ebp-0xc]` at 0x36f.

Later `collision_test_line` calls reuse `&collision`, so a direct `collision.t` read cannot stand in for the
snapshot local. Exactness therefore requires preserving an uninitialized read, which WORKER_BRIEF section 5
forbids. The frame gap (0x8c against 0x80, cell census in `scratch/workers/ai.md`) cannot be landed on its own.

**Reopen criterion (unchanged):** the owner reverses the policy for this path, and a scope shape produces frame
0x80.

### `_ai_handle_editing`: skipped, exhausted (owner policy)

The only difference from exact is the two unscaled `csmemmove` byte lengths (January 0x12a/0x206). Owner commit
`603f5b41c` parked the unsafe body, and the orchestrator repeated that it must never be landed. There is no new
evidence.

**Reopen criterion:** the owner reverses that decision.

## Do-not-repeat additions

- **Ballistic:**

  - extra-use and post-call-use counts;
  - the header `point_from_line3d` inline form, which is copy-propagated;
  - the 128-variant named/unnamed sweep and the 66 operand/placement variants;
  - `#pragma optimize` letter variants.

  The only lever that yields January's hold prefix is L-B, and it is unnatural and still inexact.
- **Line of fire:** do not search headers or reference trees for a radius macro again without new first-party
  evidence.
- **Line of sight and editing:** policy questions only; no byte work is useful.
