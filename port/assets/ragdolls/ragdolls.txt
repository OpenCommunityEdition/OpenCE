# The stock bipeds' ragdolls: what port/linux/game/ragdoll_bodies.c makes
# from each model's nodes and collision pieces, adjusted. The format is in
# that file's comment; the masses are kilograms, the angles degrees, the
# sizes world units (3.048 metres each). A model without a section is made
# as it is, weighing a marine's 80 kg.
#
# A break's damage is what an explosion must do near a limb to tear it off
# (game.dismemberment): a frag grenade's 120 all at its centre, less with
# each unit away, to nothing at 2.5. Hunters, the flood's carriers and
# infection forms, the engineers and the sentinels tear nothing.
